Mission Statement

To build sovereign digital infrastructure that a single independent engineer can own outright — architecture, data, and identity — rather than rent from platforms that can revoke access, sell the data, or disappear. Every service exists because an equivalent commercial product was judged inadequate, extractive, or untrustworthy: permit tracking not beholden to a vendor, terrain intelligence computed from physical law instead of licensed from a mapping monopoly, a browser that doesn't phone home, a community news outlet whose revenue stays in the community it serves.

The long-term purpose is broader than any one repository: a civic-technology substrate for a future where digital and physical infrastructure are the same infrastructure — where augmented and virtual reality are how people collaboratively build their own cities, and autonomous and robotic systems join that build-out under the same sovereign, auditable, provenance-tracked standards documented in this catalog.

Why It Exists

Independent verification, under duress, that a single person's engineering output can constitute a serious body of work — architected, tested, and documented to a standard that survives scrutiny, not a portfolio of toy projects.

How It's Built

Every repository is cross-referenced through a shared phase-tracking protocol, a common attribution and license standard, and a structural interconnection point (the XML intelligence pipeline) that every other service writes through.

What It Proves

Real, running, tested code: Rust unit test suites, live-provisioned cloud databases, TypeScript type-checked across two build targets, and a security/dependency posture audited and modernized on a fixed date, not a resume claim.

Where It's Going

From desktop dashboards to a mixed-reality, physically-integrated civic build-out — the architecture in this catalog is the substrate that transition is built on top of, not a rewrite that transition will require.

Section III
Novel Design Systems — Service Deliverables

Original engineering patterns designed and implemented within this estate, generalizable beyond it, and presented here as standalone service deliverables in their own right.

01
5D Cell Addressing Model MacroHarder
Extends the two-dimensional spreadsheet cell — ⟨column, row⟩ — into a five-coordinate address space: ⟨column, row, layer, tick, escape⟩. Layer gives volumetric (sheet-of-sheets) depth; tick and escape reserve temporal versioning and directional-write routing as first-class coordinates.
02
Capability-Gated MCP Module Host MacroHarder
A module registry that validates every cross-service tool call against a declared manifest before dispatch, enforcing read-only / read-write capability classes per module.
03
SOV-RULES Taxonomy Guard SQLXML
A versioned XML taxonomy gate enforced immediately before every structural database write, so schema drift and malformed writes are rejected at the boundary.
04
21-Day Cycle Governor + n+6 Stabilizer SQLXML
A self-resetting operational cycle (day-22 wipe logic) paired with a trailing 6-day recursive debugger that scopes recent history specifically to catch structural drift before it compounds.
05
Sovereign Provenance & Watermarking Suite RoadMaps
A four-part IP-forensics toolkit: BLAKE3 structural content fingerprinting, source-embedded canary tokens, DNS beacon call-home tokens, and an AES-256-GCM append-only provenance ledger.
06
Node Certification Ledger (NCL) RoadMaps · SimCity
A three-tier append-only safety and seating ledger: Tier 1 generates signed certification hashes, Tier 2 maintains the chain-linked public record, Tier 3 runs variance-rate evaluations.
07
Albert Escaped Rays (AER) Protocol Estate-wide
A directional-write behavioral contract with a locale registry and an enforced one-read/two-write invariant, giving the estate formal consistency guarantees for cross-service writes.
08
3D Design Standard & Weighted Audit Scoring MacroHarder
A category-weighted PASS / WARN / FAIL compliance scorer for adjustable-UI design-token systems — the quality gate every dashboard preset is measured against.
09
Cross-Repo Phase Synchronization Protocol Estate-wide
A shared, machine-readable roadmap schema aggregated into a single master roadmap — giving eleven services one consistent source of truth for phase state and dependencies.
10
Autonomous IP-Infringement Detection Pipeline IP-Forensics
Scanning across eight public package ecosystems, combining verbatim-copy hashing, structural AST comparison, and unique-identifier detection to surface unauthorized derivative use.
11
Append-Only Versioned Fact Model RoadMaps
A business-data schema where every fact is a permanent, superseded-not-deleted record carrying source classification, effective date, and provenance pointers.
12
GitHub-Actions-Independent Estate Pipeline RoadMaps · Estate-wide
An eight-stage CI, security, IP-enforcement, and merge-readiness pipeline running as plain Python on a cron cadence — proving full CI/CD discipline without proprietary platform runners.
13
MacroHard 3D/5D Planner Workbook Engine MacroHarder
A working 3D/5D data-planning tool with Sphere Planner mode, Parallel Grid depth slicing, and a sandboxed formula evaluator plotting algebraic surfaces directly in volumetric space.

Vision — The Physical-Digital Merger

The desktop dashboard and sovereign browser are the current interface, not the permanent one.
